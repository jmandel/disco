## Standing open questions
1. Should the pack cover `error_user` / `visual_user` (ledger #16)? `check.ts` honors `DISCO_SWAG_USER` for the slow-user steps, so a first look is one env var away.
2. Is muting the Backtrace hosts in scope (tool-side: mark ambient / block)? It would turn the locked-out refusal from a 3.5s settle into a ~40ms one without `until` and stop the 401 sentinel noise (friction.md #7–#9).
